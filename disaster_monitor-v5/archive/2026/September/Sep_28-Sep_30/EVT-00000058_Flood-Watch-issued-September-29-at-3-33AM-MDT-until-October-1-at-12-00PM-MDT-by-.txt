DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WHAT...Flooding caused by excessive rainfall continues to be
possible.

* WHERE...Portions of east central and northeast Colorado, including
the following areas: the southern Front Range Foothills, the Palmer
Divide, and adjacent plains through eastern Elbert and Lincoln
counties.

* WHEN...From late tonight through Thursday morning.

* IMPACTS...Excessive runoff may result in flooding of rivers,
creeks, streams, and other low-lying and flood-prone locations.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...
- A surge of tropical moisture from the remnants of Hurricane Polo
and a storm system will bring a threat of flooding into our area
late tonight through Thursday morning. Periods of heavy rainfall
may lead to totals of 1 to 2.5 inches in the watch area, with
locally higher amounts possible.

INSTRUCTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You should monitor later forecasts and be alert for possible Flood
Warnings. Those living in areas prone to flooding should be prepared
to take action should flooding develop.

Turn around, don't drown when encountering flooded roads. Most flood
deaths occur in vehicles.
