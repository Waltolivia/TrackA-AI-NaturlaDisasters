AFFECTED AREAS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Catalina and Santa Barbara Islands; Ventura County Beaches; Malibu Coast; Los Angeles County Beaches

DESCRIPTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* WHAT...For the Beach Hazards Statement, dangerous rip currents
and breaking waves due to elevated surf. For the Coastal Flood
Advisory, moderate coastal flooding expected. For the High
Surf Advisory, breaking waves of 4 to 7 feet with dangerous
rip currents.

* WHERE...Catalina and Santa Barbara Islands, Ventura County
Beaches, Malibu Coast and Los Angeles County Beaches.

* WHEN...Until 5 PM PDT Tuesday.

* IMPACTS...Moderate flooding of sea water is likely over some
vulnerable low-lying coastal areas, especially around the time
of high tide, including over beach parking lots, bike paths,
and harbor walkways. Moderate to locally significant beach
erosion is likely. Very localized damage possible to most
vulnerable areas. There is an increased risk for ocean
drowning. Rip currents can pull swimmers and surfers out to
sea. Large breaking waves can cause injury, wash people off
beaches and rocks, and capsize small boats near shore.

* ADDITIONAL DETAILS...Areas of most concern are Avalon, Long
Beach (Peninsula and Belmont Shore). While the surf will
likely be smaller than Marie (from earlier this month), the
lingering erosion effects enhance the vulnerabilities for
locally more significant coastal flooding.

INSTRUCTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Take the necessary actions to protect flood-prone property. Do
NOT drive around barricades or through water of unknown depth.

Remain out of the water due to dangerous surf conditions, or stay
near occupied lifeguard towers. Rock jetties can be deadly in
such conditions, stay off the rocks.

Remain out of the water due to hazardous swimming conditions, or
stay near occupied lifeguard towers. Rock jetties can be deadly
in such conditions, stay off the rocks.
